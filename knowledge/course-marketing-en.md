# Digital Marketer course

## Who the course is for

- Beginners who want to master an in-demand digital profession without programming.
- Entrepreneurs who want to promote their own business online.
- SMM specialists, copywriters and managers who want to broaden their skills and earn more.

## Program by module

### Module 1. Marketing basics

Target audience and segmentation, positioning, the sales funnel, marketing strategy, media plan and budget.

### Module 2. Search and targeted advertising

Setting up ads in Yandex Direct, targeted advertising on VKontakte and Telegram Ads, writing ads, optimising campaigns.

### Module 3. SEO and content marketing

How search works, the semantic core, website optimisation, content strategy, email marketing and newsletters.

### Module 4. SMM

Promotion on social networks and Telegram, the content plan, working with bloggers, community management.

### Module 5. Web analytics

Yandex Metrica, goals and UTM tags, end-to-end analytics, calculating ROI and CAC, reports for clients. The final project is a comprehensive promotion strategy for a real business.

## Duration, format and schedule

The course lasts **5 months**. Learning is online: recorded video lessons, live webinars twice a week — on Tuesdays and Thursdays at 7 pm Moscow time, and practical assignments reviewed by a mentor. The recommended workload is 6–8 hours a week.

## Portfolio projects

During the course you will complete 4 projects:

1. A marketing strategy and media plan for an online school.
2. An advertising campaign in Yandex Direct with a real budget.
3. A content plan and promotion for a Telegram channel.
4. A final promotion strategy for a partner business.
