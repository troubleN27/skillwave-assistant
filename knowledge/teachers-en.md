# Skillwave Academy teachers

Every course has a lead teacher who designs the program, runs the live webinars and is responsible for the quality of the course. They work together with mentors who review homework. All teachers are practising specialists with at least 5 years of experience at product IT companies.

## Dmitry Orlov — Frontend Developer course

Senior frontend developer with 9 years in the profession. For the last five years he has been building the interface of a fintech service used by several million people and leads a team of six frontend developers. He specialises in React, Next.js and interface performance. On the course he teaches the React and Next.js modules and reviews the code of final projects.

## Anna Belova — UX/UI Designer course

Lead product designer with 8 years in the profession. She has designed mobile apps for delivery and online education services and built design systems from scratch. She believes a good interface starts with research, not with a picture. On the course she teaches the research and interface design modules and reviews students' designs at webinars.

## Sergey Kovalev — Python Developer course

Backend team lead with 14 years in the profession. He has built high-load services in Python and Django for e-commerce and logistics and in recent years has led a backend team. On the course he is responsible for the web development, API and infrastructure modules and runs mock technical interviews.

## Maria Lebedeva — Data Analyst course

Product analyst with 7 years in the profession. She has worked with data at a marketplace and a mobile bank: she built metric systems and launched A/B tests and dashboards for management. On the course she teaches the SQL, statistics and product analytics modules and supervises final studies.

## Timur Galiev — Digital Marketer course

Performance marketer with 10 years in the profession. He has run advertising campaigns for online schools, services and online stores and now heads a digital marketing department. On the course he teaches the advertising and web analytics modules and helps students launch their first campaign with a real budget.

## Ekaterina Smirnova — Graphic Designer course

Art director at a branding agency with 8 years in the profession. She has created brand identities for restaurants, city festivals and tech startups and led visual communications for a book publisher. On the course she teaches the typography and identity modules, reviews students' work at webinars and helps them build a portfolio.

## Mentors

In addition to the lead teachers, every course has mentors — specialists with at least 3 years of experience. They review practical assignments, usually within 48 hours, answer questions in the cohort chat and help with the final project. Each mentor works with no more than 25 students.
