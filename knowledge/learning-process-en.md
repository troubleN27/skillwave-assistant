# How studying at Skillwave Academy works

## Step 1. Request and choosing a course

Leave a request on the website or write to us on Telegram at @skillwave_academy. A manager will contact you during working hours (Monday to Friday, 10 am – 7 pm Moscow time), tell you about the programs and help you choose a profession. After your request the manager opens free access to the introductory lessons of the chosen course for 7 days, so you can try the format before paying.

## Step 2. Contract and payment

Accept the offer contract and pay for the course: in full with a 10% discount or in interest-free installments for 12 months. Access to the learning platform and the introductory module opens within 15 minutes of payment.

## Step 3. Cohort start

Studying is organised in cohorts: on the start date you and your group receive the first module of the program and join the cohort chat in Telegram. You are assigned a mentor who will review your assignments throughout the course.

## Step 4. A student's week

A typical study week:

- **recorded video lessons** — 2–3 lessons of 20–40 minutes each, watch them at any time;
- **two live webinars** at 7 pm Moscow time, about 1.5 hours each — difficult topics, answers to questions, code reviews and design critiques. The recording appears on the platform the same evening;
- **a practical assignment** on the week's topic — the mentor usually reviews it within 48 hours and gives detailed feedback;
- **discussions in the cohort chat** with the mentor and fellow students.

The recommended workload is 8–10 hours a week (6–8 hours on the "Digital Marketer" course). Deadlines are flexible: you can study at your own pace and combine studying with work. Lessons are taught in Russian.

## Step 5. Portfolio projects

In every module you complete a practical project. By the end of the course your portfolio has 4 to 6 projects, depending on the profession.

## Step 6. Final project

At the end of the course you complete a final project — on most courses on a brief from a real client — and defend it in front of the teacher.

## Step 7. Certificate and career center

After defending your final project and completing at least 80% of the practical assignments, you receive an electronic Skillwave Academy certificate. The career center helps with your CV, portfolio and interview preparation during the course and for 6 months after graduation.

## If you need a break

You can transfer to the next cohort free of charge — once per course, for up to 3 months. Access to the course materials stays forever.
