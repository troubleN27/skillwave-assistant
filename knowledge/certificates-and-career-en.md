# Certificates and career support

## Certificate of completion

Graduates receive a **Skillwave Academy certificate** listing the topics studied, the skills gained and the projects completed. The certificate is issued electronically with a unique number that an employer can use to verify it on our website.

To receive the certificate, you need to complete at least 80% of the practical assignments and defend your final project.

A certificate in English is issued free of charge on request — useful if you plan to work with companies abroad.

## Career center

Help with finding a job is included in the price of every course. The career center helps you:

- write a CV and a cover letter;
- build a portfolio on GitHub, Behance or as case studies;
- prepare for interviews: we run mock interviews, including technical ones;
- find vacancies: we send graduates' CVs to our partner companies.

Career center consultations are available during the course and for **6 months after graduation**.

## Internships and partners

We work with more than 150 IT companies and digital agencies. The best students receive internship invitations while still studying. On most courses the final projects are done on briefs from real clients.

## Graduate results

- 78% of graduates find a job in their new profession within 6 months of finishing the course.
- The average time to find the first job is 3 months.

Getting a job also depends on the graduate's own effort, so we do not guarantee employment, but we help at every stage of the job search.
