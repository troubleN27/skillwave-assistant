## Payment methods

- Bank card: Visa, Mastercard, MIR.
- SBP (Faster Payments System).
- Bank transfer for legal entities — if a company pays for the training. Write to a manager at hello@skillwave.academy and we will prepare a contract and an invoice.
- Installments through partner banks.

After payment you receive a receipt and the offer contract by email, and access to the platform opens within 15 minutes.

## Installments

- Interest-free installments (0%) for up to 12 months — the school pays the bank's interest.
- The first payment is due one month after the application.
- The application is completed online in 5–10 minutes and only requires a Russian passport. The bank's decision arrives within a few minutes.
- Early repayment is possible at any time without a fee.
- If you are not a Russian citizen, you can pay the school directly in two or three parts. Please check the details with a manager.

## Discounts

- **10%** off when you pay for the whole course at once, in a single payment.
- **15%** off a second course for our students and graduates.
- Discounts cannot be combined: the one that is best for you applies.
- Seasonal promotions and special offers are published on the website and in our Telegram channel @skillwave_academy.

## Payment by a company

If your employer pays for the training, we sign a contract with the legal entity, issue an invoice and provide the closing documents. Corporate groups of 5 people or more get individual terms.
