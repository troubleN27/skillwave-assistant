## Payment methods

- Bank card: Visa, Mastercard, MIR.
- SBP (Faster Payments System).
- Bank transfer for companies, based on a contract and an invoice.
- Installments through partner banks.

## Installments

- Interest-free installments for up to 12 months — the school covers the bank's interest.
- The first payment is due one month after the application.
- The application takes 5–10 minutes online and requires a Russian passport.
- Students who are not Russian citizens can pay the school directly in two or three parts. Please contact a manager for details.

## Discounts

- **10%** off when you pay for the whole course at once.
- **15%** off a second course for our students and graduates.
- Discounts cannot be combined; the best one for you applies.
