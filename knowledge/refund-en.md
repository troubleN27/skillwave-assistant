# Refund policy

## Full refund within the first 14 days

If within the first 14 calendar days of your studies you realise the course is not right for you, we will return **100% of the amount paid**. You do not need to explain why.

The 14 days are counted from the cohort start date, or from the payment date if you paid after the cohort had already started.

## Refund after 14 days

After the first 14 days we refund **the modules you have not started yet**. The cost of modules you have completed or started is withheld in proportion to their share of the course program.

For example, if the program has 6 modules and you have completed 2 of them, we refund the cost of the remaining 4 modules.

## Refund for installment payments

If the course was paid in installments through a partner bank, the school returns the money to the bank and the loan is closed or recalculated. Any amount you have already paid the bank above the cost of the completed part is returned to you.

## How to request a refund

1. Send a request to hello@skillwave.academy from the email address used for the purchase.
2. Include your full name, the course name and the details for the refund (or the details of the card used for payment).
3. A manager will confirm receipt of the request within 1 working day.

The money arrives in your account within **10 working days** of receiving the request.

## Postponing instead of a refund

If you need a break — because of work, a move or any other reason — you can transfer to the next cohort free of charge. A pause is available once per course for up to 3 months.
