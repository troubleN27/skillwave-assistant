# Frequently asked questions

## Which course should a beginner choose?

All our courses are designed for beginners. If you want to program and build websites, choose "Frontend Developer". If you prefer visual work and design, choose "UX/UI Designer". If you like logic and want to write the server side, choose "Python Developer". If you enjoy working with numbers, choose "Data Analyst". If you want to start earning as soon as possible without programming, choose "Digital Marketer" — it is the shortest course (5 months). If you are unsure, leave a request and a manager will help you decide.

## Do I need experience or a special education?

No. All courses start from the basics; you only need to be comfortable using a computer. Higher mathematics and a technical education are not required.

## How much time do I need for studying?

On average 8–10 hours a week, and 6–8 hours on the "Digital Marketer" course. You can combine studying with work: recorded lessons are available at any time and webinars take place in the evening.

## What time are the webinars?

Live webinars take place twice a week at 7 pm Moscow time and last about 1.5 hours. If you cannot attend, the recording appears on the platform the same evening.

## What if I can't keep up with the group?

You can study at your own pace: deadlines are flexible and a mentor will help you make an individual plan. If you need a break, you can transfer to the next cohort free of charge — once per course, for up to 3 months.

## How is homework reviewed?

Assignments are reviewed by a mentor — a practising specialist. A review usually takes up to 48 hours; you get detailed comments and can improve your work.

## Are installments available?

Yes, interest-free installments for up to 12 months through partner banks. The first payment is due one month after the application. For example, the "UX/UI Designer" course in installments costs from 5,750 RUB per month.

## Are there any discounts?

10% off when you pay for the whole course at once and 15% off a second course for students and graduates. Discounts cannot be combined.

## Can I get a refund?

Yes. In the first 14 days of studying we refund 100% of the amount; later we refund the cost of the modules you have not started yet. The money arrives within 10 working days of the request.

## Do you issue a certificate?

Yes, graduates receive an electronic Skillwave Academy certificate with a unique number for verification. To get it, you need to complete at least 80% of the practical assignments and defend your final project.

## Do you help with finding a job?

Yes, help is included in the price: the career center helps with your CV and portfolio, runs mock interviews and recommends graduates to partner companies. Support continues for 6 months after graduation.

## Do you guarantee employment?

We do not guarantee employment, because the result also depends on the graduate's own effort. At the same time, 78% of graduates find a job in their new profession within 6 months of the course.

## How long do I keep access to the materials?

Access to lessons, webinar recordings and course materials stays forever, including all future program updates.

## When do the next cohorts start?

Digital Marketer — 15 December 2026, Frontend Developer — 19 January 2027, UX/UI Designer — 26 January 2027, Python Developer — 2 February 2027, Data Analyst — 9 February 2027.

## Can I study from another country?

Yes, studying is fully online, so you can study from any country. You need a computer and a stable internet connection. Webinars follow Moscow time, and their recordings are always available. Lessons are taught in Russian.

## What computer do I need?

Any laptop or desktop on Windows, macOS or Linux made in the last 5–6 years with 8 GB of RAM will do. All the software used in the courses is free.

## Can I try before buying?

Yes, after you leave a request a manager will open free access to the introductory lessons of the chosen course for 7 days.

## Can a company pay for my training?

Yes, we sign a contract with the legal entity, issue an invoice and provide the closing documents. Groups of 5 people or more get individual terms.
