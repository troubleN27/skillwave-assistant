# About Skillwave Academy

## Who we are

Skillwave Academy is an online school of digital professions. Since 2019 we have been helping people learn a new profession from scratch or strengthen their current skills and move to the next level. More than 12,000 students have studied with us so far.

We teach six professions:

- Frontend Developer — 9 months;
- UX/UI Designer — 6 months;
- Graphic Designer — 7 months;
- Python Developer — 10 months;
- Data Analyst — 8 months;
- Digital Marketer — 5 months.

All courses are designed for beginners: no special background is required, you only need to be comfortable using a computer and have 8–10 hours a week for studying. Lessons, webinars and mentor feedback are in Russian.

## How the learning works

Learning is fully online on our learning platform. Every course combines:

- **recorded video lessons** — watch them whenever it suits you and rewatch as often as you like;
- **live webinars** twice a week at 7 pm Moscow time — difficult topics, answers to questions, live code reviews and design critiques. Webinar recordings appear on the platform the same evening;
- **practical assignments** after every topic — a mentor reviews them and gives detailed feedback, usually within 48 hours;
- **portfolio projects** — in every course students complete real projects they later show to employers;
- **a cohort chat** in Telegram where you can ask the mentor and your fellow students.

You can study from any country: all you need is a computer and a stable internet connection. Access to the course materials, including program updates, is kept forever.

## Teachers and mentors

Courses are taught by practising specialists with at least 5 years of experience at product IT companies: developers, designers, analysts and marketers. Teachers run the webinars and keep the program up to date; mentors review homework and support students throughout the course.

Each mentor works with no more than 25 students, so everyone gets attention and detailed comments on their work.

## How to start

1. Choose a course or leave a request — a manager will help you pick a profession.
2. Sign the contract and pay for the course in a convenient way (in full or in installments).
3. Get access to the platform and the introductory module right after payment.
4. Start studying with your cohort on the start date.
