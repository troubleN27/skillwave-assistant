# About Skillwave Academy

## Who we are

Skillwave Academy is an online school of digital professions. Since 2019 we have helped more than 12,000 students learn a new profession from scratch or level up their current skills.

We teach five professions:

- Frontend Developer — 9 months;
- UX/UI Designer — 6 months;
- Python Developer — 10 months;
- Data Analyst — 8 months;
- Digital Marketer — 5 months.

All courses are designed for beginners: no prior experience is required, you only need to be comfortable using a computer and have 8–10 hours a week for studying. Classes are taught in Russian.

## How the learning works

Learning is fully online on our learning platform. Every course combines:

- **recorded video lessons** you can watch at any time;
- **live webinars** twice a week at 7 pm Moscow time, with recordings available the same evening;
- **practical assignments** reviewed by a mentor, usually within 48 hours;
- **portfolio projects**, including a final project for a real client;
- **a group chat** in Telegram with mentors and fellow students.

You can study from any country. Access to course materials, including future updates, is unlimited.

## Teachers and mentors

Courses are taught by practitioners with at least 5 years of experience at product IT companies. Each mentor works with no more than 25 students.
