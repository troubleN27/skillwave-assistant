# Data Analyst course

## Who the course is for

- Beginners who want to work with data and help companies make decisions based on numbers.
- Economists, marketers and managers who work a lot in Excel and want to master professional tools.
- People who want to enter IT through analytics without deep programming.

You do not need higher mathematics: we cover all the statistics you need from scratch.

## Program by module

### Module 1. Excel and Google Sheets

Formulas and functions, pivot tables, data cleaning, visualisation, basics of financial and product analysis.

### Module 2. SQL

Database queries, filtering, grouping and aggregation, joining tables, window functions, working with PostgreSQL and ClickHouse.

### Module 3. Python for data analysis

Python basics, the pandas and NumPy libraries, data processing and cleaning, visualisation with matplotlib and seaborn, working in Jupyter Notebook.

### Module 4. Statistics and A/B tests

Descriptive statistics, distributions, hypothesis testing, designing and analysing A/B tests.

### Module 5. Product analytics and BI

Product metrics (retention, LTV, conversion, cohort analysis), building dashboards in DataLens and Power BI, unit economics.

### Module 6. Final project and career

An analytical study on real data from a partner company, presenting the findings, CV and portfolio preparation, mock interviews.

## Duration, format and schedule

The course lasts **8 months**. Learning is online: recorded video lessons, live webinars twice a week — on Mondays and Wednesdays at 7 pm Moscow time, and practical assignments reviewed by a mentor. The recommended workload is 8–10 hours a week.

## Portfolio projects

During the course you will complete 5 projects:

1. An online store sales analysis in Excel.
2. A user base study with SQL.
3. An analysis of mobile app user behaviour in Python.
4. A dashboard of key product metrics.
5. A final study on data from a partner company.
