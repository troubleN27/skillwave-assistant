# Graphic Designer course

## Who the course is for

- Beginners who want to learn graphic design from scratch and create brand identities, packaging, posters and social media visuals.
- SMM specialists, marketers and editors who already make their own visuals and want to do it professionally.
- Illustrators and photographers who want to earn money from commercial design.

You do not need to be able to draw by hand: we teach everything you need — composition, colour, type — during the course.

## Program by module

### Module 1. Graphic design basics

Composition, modular grids, colour and contrast, working with images, building visual taste and finding references.

### Module 2. Typography and layout

Choosing and pairing typefaces, text hierarchy, laying out posters, booklets and presentations, preparing files for print.

### Module 3. Identity and branding

Logo, brand identity and brand guidelines, brand applications: business cards, letterheads, merchandise, signage.

### Module 4. Digital design and packaging

Visuals for social media and advertising, banners, packaging and label design, mockups and presenting work to a client.

### Module 5. Final project and career

A brand identity for a real client from brief to guidelines, Behance case studies, portfolio and CV, freelance and agency work, mock interviews.

## Duration, format and schedule

The course lasts **7 months**. Learning is online: recorded video lessons, live webinars twice a week — on Tuesdays and Thursdays at 7 pm Moscow time, and work reviews with a mentor. The recommended workload is 8–10 hours a week. We work in Figma and Adobe Illustrator; while studying you can use the free versions and alternatives we recommend in the first lesson.

## Portfolio projects

During the course you will complete 5 projects:

1. A series of posters for a city festival.
2. A logo and brand identity for a coffee shop.
3. Packaging design for a product line.
4. Visuals for a social media ad campaign.
5. A final project: a brand identity with guidelines on a brief from a real client.
